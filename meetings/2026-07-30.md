## Meeting Notes

### This Week in HLSL

We saw 29 PRs merged, 25 new PRs opened, and have 64 PRs ongoing.

For a full list of merged PRs, see the appendix.

### Math intrinsics and double overloads

We discussed some inconsistencies around intrinsics that are pure math and
whether or not they have double overloads or if using doubles for these casts
down to a float. We need to survey DXC's implementation here to get an idea of
how prevalent these are, but it would probably be a good thing to make a change
in HLSL 202x to add double overloads for most or all of these.
