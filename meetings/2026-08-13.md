## Meeting Notes

### This Week in HLSL

This week had 33 PRs merged in HLSL:

- **HLSL Frontend**
  - Various intrinsics now support matrices
  - The GSOC clangd patches are starting to land
  - Documentation improvements
  - Several HLSL functions are now implemented in the HLSL headers
- **DirectX Backend**
  - Lowering for pixel shader constructs and textures has had quite a bit of
    progress
- **SPIR-V Backend**
  - Legalization and lowering improvements - specifically around aggregates
  - Many cleanups and NFC refactorings
- **Offload Test Suite**
  - XFAIL cleanups and infrastructure improvements
  
However, there were 27 new PRs opened. We are, however, down to 49 ongoing open
PRs after some cleanup of some of the older PRs that are no longer relevant.

For a full list of merged PRs, see the appendix.
