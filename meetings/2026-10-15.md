### What's Happening in HLSL

From Octbober 8th to Octbober 15th there were 34 New PRs and 38 PRs merged in HLSL-related
areas.

#### HLSL Frontend
- Added Barrier builtin and isfinite() intrinsic.
- Added semantic signature packing flags, packing invocation, and semantic validations.

#### DirectX Backend
- None.

#### Offload Test Suite
- Updated/removed XFAILs for Barrier, isfinite, WaveGetLaneCount, and SPIR-V legalization.

#### SPIR-V Backend
- Implemented WaveGetLaneCount and llvm.minimum/maximum lowering, and decomposed bool vector bitcasts element-wise.
- Fixed crash on dynamic-offset byte buffer vector access and stopped caching SyncScope IDs across contexts.
- Various cleanups, bug fixes, and non-shader feature additions.

#### Other
- Added GlobalISel nomerge attribute support.
- Various cleanups.

There are 64 ongoing PRs.

For a full list of merged PRs, see the appendix.
